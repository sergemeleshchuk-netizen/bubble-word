TASK: rank the words of ONE category by how obviously each one belongs to it.

AUDIENCE: an average American adult casual player of a word puzzle. They see four
word bubbles and must realize the four belong together. They are not a specialist.

CATEGORY:
  key:   $category_key
  label: $label
  rule:  $rule

WORDS (with every OTHER category the base already puts each word in — that is what
competes for the player's first recall):
$words_json

WHAT obviousness MEANS HERE
It is not "is this correct" — every word below is already correct. It is one question:
**when the player reads this word, is THIS the meaning that comes to mind first?**

  1.00-0.90  the word means this and essentially nothing else to a casual player
  0.89-0.75  a leading meaning; a competing sense exists but does not win
  0.74-0.60  correct and recognizable, but another meaning arrives first
  0.59-0.40  the player needs the category label in front of them to accept it
  0.39-0.00  regional, dated, jargon, or knowable only to someone in the field

WHAT TO WEIGH
1. Competing senses that arrive FIRST. `gym` is a real American school subject, but a
   player reads it as a building before a class period. `crane` is a bird and a machine.
2. A word can sit in many categories and still be obvious in this one. `apple` is in
   eleven categories and is still a perfect FRUIT; `orange` is a perfect COLOR AND a
   perfect FRUIT. Count of other homes is NOT the signal — which meaning wins is.
3. Cultural and regional narrowness. A word obvious inside American schooling, American
   sports or one US region is not obvious to the average player.
4. Words that only work once the label is visible score low, even if they are textbook
   members of the category.

HARD REQUIREMENT: SPREAD THEM
The scores you are replacing were filled in one value for the whole category, which is
why this pass exists. If your output gives every word the same or nearly the same score,
you have not done the task. Real categories have a top and a bottom. Find them.
Use the full range you believe in — do not compress everything into 0.8-0.9.

OUTPUT: raw JSON only, no markdown, exactly this shape, one entry per word given:
{
  "category_key": "$category_key",
  "grades": [
    {
      "word": "math",
      "obviousness_score": 0.97,
      "reason": "One meaning only; the first thing anyone names as a school subject."
    },
    {
      "word": "gym",
      "obviousness_score": 0.55,
      "reason": "Reads as the room, not the class period; the subject sense is American schoolspeak."
    }
  ]
}
