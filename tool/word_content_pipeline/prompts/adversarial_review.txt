TASK: act as a strict critic of proposed word-category memberships for an American English
word puzzle. Your job is to find reasons a membership should NOT be shipped, not to defend it.

AUDIENCE the content must serve: an average American adult casual player.

MEMBERSHIPS TO REVIEW:
$memberships_json

HOW TO JUDGE
1. Read the category rule literally. If the word only fits the label but not the rule, reject it.
2. Reject subjective, evaluative or vague memberships.
3. Reject loose metaphorical associations.
4. Check the declared sense: if the reason relies on a different sense than sense_definition, flag it.
5. Check the scores. fit_score is correctness; obviousness_score is how fast a casual player sees it.
   Overrated obviousness is a common error — correct it downward without changing fit_score.
6. Use "hard_only" when the membership is correct but too non-obvious for easy levels.
7. Use "manual_review" when you genuinely cannot decide from the data given.
8. Prefer rejecting a doubtful entry over approving it. Do not be generous.

OUTPUT: raw JSON only, no markdown, exactly this shape:
{
  "verdicts": [
    {
      "membership_id": 123,
      "recommended_decision": "approve",
      "corrected_fit_score": 0.95,
      "corrected_obviousness_score": 0.7,
      "issues": ["The category is broader than the provided rule"],
      "explanation": "One or two sentences of reasoning."
    }
  ]
}
